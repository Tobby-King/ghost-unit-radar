Ghost Unit Radar

MapHow it worksMethods & Ethics

What this is: a data-quality triage tool that helps INEC, accredited observers and journalists decide where to send field checks. It scores whether each polling unit is physically plausible, based on where it is and how many people live around it. It does not detect fraud. Most flags will have innocent causes, and every flag shows one.

The three checks

1. Location integrity REAL DATA

Every published coordinate is tested against Nigeria's state boundaries. A coordinate is flagged if it is outside Nigeria, in a different state from the one INEC lists (points within km of the border are allowed, because the boundaries are simplified), or stacked, meaning or more units share one exact point, which shows a ward or LGA centroid was used instead of a GPS fix.

2. Population plausibility WORLDPOP + SYNTHETIC REGISTER

For each unit with a usable coordinate we estimate the adults living within km. We take WorldPop's satellite-derived 2020 population density, multiply by the circle's area, then by an adult (18+) share of . Nigeria's population is very young: about 43% are under 15 and 6–7% are 15–17 (UN WPP). We divide that by the number of polling units in the same circle to get the adults this unit plausibly serves, then compare it with the (synthetic) register. A register above 1.2× is amber; above 2× or with almost no residents it is red. Units with an unreliable coordinate (outside Nigeria, wrong state, stacked) are not scored on population, because their catchment would be meaningless.

3. Clock audit SYNTHETIC RESULT SHEETS

One BVAS device takes roughly 30–120 seconds per voter. The maximum number of voters a unit can accredit is polling hours × 3600 ÷ seconds per voter × devices. A sheet is impossible if it exceeds this at the fastest 30 s, and implausible if it exceeds it at the typical speed (default 45 s, adjustable on the map from 30 to 120 s).

Validation on synthetic anomalies

We planted anomalies of known type (30 per state with coordinates) in the synthetic registers and result sheets. The ground truth is in data/out/planted_ground_truth.csv.

Some flags are not planted (see the last row of the table). They arise from the synthetic generator and the coarse 1 km population grid, mostly in sparse rural areas. Real-world false alarms of the same kind are expected, which is why every flag goes to field verification.

Sensitivity analysis

We reran the pipeline at three radii and five BVAS speeds (pipeline/sensitivity.py). A smaller radius produces many more false alarms in sparse areas. A larger one dilutes real anomalies. We use 1 km. The clock result depends heavily on the assumed speed, which is why a sheet is only called "impossible" at the fastest 30 s.

Responsible use and ethics

  • No INEC system is accessed. No IReV or voter-registration queries are made. Only static public files are used.
  • No personal data. The tool works at polling-unit level. No voter names or numbers are used or generated.
  • Synthetic registers. Every registered-voter and result-sheet number is generated and labelled SYNTHETIC wherever it appears. With real INEC data the same code would produce real scores. That should only happen under INEC's authority.
  • Non-partisan. No party, candidate or result data is used. Scores are the same for every unit regardless of politics.
  • "Needs verification", never "fraud". Flags are leads, and each comes with its most likely innocent cause. The 2022 coordinates are mostly geocoded, not surveyed, so location flags mainly show where INEC's published map needs cleaning.
  • Explainable, not a black box. Every score is a sum of named, documented penalties. There is no hidden model.

Limitations

  • The polling-unit list is a 2022 snapshot. INEC may have corrected coordinates since then.
  • The 1 km population grid smooths dense neighbourhoods and misses very new settlements. The Earth Engine script (earth_engine/catchment_export.js) upgrades this to WorldPop 100 m age-structured data plus Open Buildings counts.
  • The adult share is a national constant. Regional age structures differ.
  • A fixed radius is a simple catchment. Real units serve irregular areas, and rural units often draw voters from further away.
  • State boundaries are simplified. Units near borders are given a km tolerance.
  • LGA-level and water-body checks are not yet included.
